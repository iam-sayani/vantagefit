01 · Foundations

Brand principles

Every page should feel optimistic enough for wellbeing and credible enough for an enterprise buying decision.

01

Human clarity

Use natural language, achievable activities, generous type, and familiar patterns.

02

Evidence with restraint

Make outcomes visible, source claims, and avoid unsupported health or ROI promises.

03

Inclusive momentum

Show different ages, abilities, roles, and ways to participate—not athletic performance alone.

09 · Content

Concrete, credible language

Write primarily to the HR buyer while showing employee value. Translate every capability into a business or human outcome.

Do

  • “See what is working—not just what was launched.”
  • Use concise US English.
  • Source customer results and statistics.
  • Use “wellbeing” consistently.
  • Name the next step in every CTA.

Don’t

  • “Revolutionize your wellness journey.”
  • Invent outcomes, integrations, or certifications.
  • Describe medical or guaranteed savings claims.
  • Repeat “Learn more” across every card.
  • Make participation sound athletic or exclusive.

10 · Accessibility

Accessibility is part of the brand

Design to WCAG 2.2 AA as the minimum. Favor enhanced contrast for body text and controls used by older employees.

AA

Core requirements

  • Normal text: at least 4.5:1 contrast; aim for 7:1 where practical.
  • Large text and essential UI boundaries: at least 3:1.
  • Keyboard-visible focus, semantic headings, labels, and status text.
  • Touch targets: at least 44 × 44px; preferred mobile CTA height: 56px.
  • Respect reduced motion and never communicate status using color alone.
