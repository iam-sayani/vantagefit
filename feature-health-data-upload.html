How it works in Vantage Fit

Upload

Employees upload, AI reads, patterns roll up

An employee uploads a lab report in the app and it is parsed automatically. Nobody at your company handles a document or a data entry step.

Dashboard

One dashboard for population health

HR admins see aggregated insights in the Workforce Health Dashboard: a population overview, trends over time, and deficiency patterns for vitamin D, iron, cholesterol, and more.

Action

Insights into action

A workforce-wide vitamin D pattern becomes the case for outdoor activity challenges; a cholesterol trend becomes the case for a nutrition program.

Privacy is the architecture, not a policy line

HR sees aggregated patterns; individual results stay private to the employee. No admin can open a person's lab report or trace a trend back to a name. That is what keeps this inside a wellness program and out of clinical territory.
