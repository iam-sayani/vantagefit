Participation reports show activity, not outcomes. Lab report insights show workforce health actually changing, in aggregate, while every individual result stays with its owner.

How lab report insights work

01

Employees upload

An employee uploads a lab report in the app. Nobody at your company handles a document or a data entry step.

02

AI reads the report

The report is parsed automatically. The employee sees their own results in full detail, and only they can.

03

Patterns roll up

Results aggregate into workforce-level patterns in the Workforce Health Dashboard: a population overview, trends over time, and deficiency patterns for vitamin D, iron, cholesterol, and more.

Privacy is the architecture, not a policy line

HR sees aggregated patterns; individual results stay private to the employee. No admin can open a person's lab report or trace a trend back to a name. That is what keeps this inside a wellness program and out of clinical territory.
