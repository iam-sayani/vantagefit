Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Single Challenge and Unlimited Challenges?

Single Challenge is a one-time, 4-week wellness event. It suits a focused campaign or a pilot. Unlimited Challenges is a year-round subscription: run as many challenges as you like throughout the year.

What is the difference between Basic and Pro?

Basic covers step challenges with participant leaderboards. Pro adds walking, running, cycling, yoga, and more, plus team leaderboards, a dedicated account manager, detailed analytics, and single sign-on and HR system integrations.

How is pricing calculated?

Pricing is based on your total employee count, with volume discounts applied automatically. Use the slider or type your employee count to see your estimate in real time.

Can we upgrade from Single Challenge to Unlimited Challenges?

Yes. Your data, configurations, and challenge history carry over, and we prorate any upgrade mid-period.

Can we upgrade from Basic to Pro?

Yes, at any time, with proration applied to the remaining period.

How long does go-live take?

Basic takes about 2 weeks with self-guided onboarding. Pro takes about 3 weeks and includes hands-on setup and a customized communications plan.

Do we get a dedicated account manager?

Pro includes a dedicated account manager with hands-on support through launch and beyond. Basic includes email onboarding and access to the help center.

What devices and integrations are supported?

All plans connect with Fitbit, Garmin, Apple Health, Google Fit, and more. Pro adds single sign-on and HR system integrations for employee data sync.

Is the platform available in multiple languages?

Yes. Vantage Fit runs in 14+ languages on every plan, which makes it a fit for global and regional teams.

Do you offer a trial or pilot?

Yes. Book a demo and we will walk you through a pilot plan sized to your organization and goals.
