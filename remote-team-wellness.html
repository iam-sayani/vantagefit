Frequently asked questions

How do you cater to different time zones?

Vantage Fit caters to different time zones by scheduling live fitness sessions and challenges accessible at various times, offering on-demand webinar sessions, and providing flexibility for users to set their own challenge start times.

Can we integrate our own virtual fitness instructors?

Currently, Vantage Fit does not offer this feature within its app, but it is a potential enhancement that may be enabled in the future.

What tech requirements are needed for remote employees to participate?

Basic tech requirements include a stable internet connection, a compatible device (smartphone, tablet, or computer), and the ability to access our platform through a web browser or our mobile app.
