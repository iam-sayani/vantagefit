A leaderboard your skeptics can audit

When rewards ride on steps, the count has to be trustworthy. Five checks keep the leaderboard honest.

  • Trusted sources only

    Only recognized apps and devices count. Unknown sources are rejected.

  • One device at a time

    One primary source per person, so nothing double-counts.

  • Anomaly and GPS checks

    Implausible spikes and non-walking GPS are filtered out.

  • Three-day results buffer

    Winners are confirmed after a three-day sync window.

  • Manual entry off by default

    Typed-in steps stay off unless you enable them.
