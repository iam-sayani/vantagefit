One Virtual Marathon.

Organize a one-day virtual marathon for your global workforce with Vantage Fit. Unify teams across geographies, boost engagement, and promote wellness—all at once.

Book a demoSee pricing

Custom distances, global connection, instant rewards

01

Global participation

  • Employees from different time zones and regions can participate in the same event, fostering inclusivity and collaboration.
  • Our platform automatically adjusts for time zone differences, ensuring all participants feel connected.
  • Vantage Fit's Virtual Marathon is designed for simplicity—minimal setup with maximum impact.

02

Live leaderboard

  • The live leaderboard keeps participants engaged with real-time updates on rankings, progress, and achievements.
  • Encourage camaraderie and competition by showcasing individual and team standings.
  • Dynamic updates motivate employees to push their limits and stay active during the event.

03

Multiple distance goals

  • Employees can choose between 5 km, 10 km, or 21 km challenges, ensuring inclusivity for all fitness levels.
  • Each participant earns points based on the distance they cover, encouraging personal achievements while contributing to team success.
  • Flexible distance goals make the event accessible to everyone, from beginners to seasoned runners.

04

Recognition through badges and incentivization

  • Participants earn badges for completing milestones (e.g., 5 km, 10 km, 21 km), creating a sense of accomplishment.
  • HR managers can customize rewards through gift cards, wellness perks, or company specific incentives to align with organizational goals.
  • Recognition fosters a culture of appreciation, boosting morale and long-term engagement.
