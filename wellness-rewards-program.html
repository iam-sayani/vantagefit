Frequently asked questions

How can we reward employees for their wellness activities?

Employees receive rewards for their wellness activities through automated badges and certifications, with multiple badge categories corresponding to different levels of achievement.

Are there any options for monetary rewards available within Vantage Fit?

Vantage Fit provides Fit points based on completed activities, which can be utilized to redeem gift cards across over 20 categories.

What is the value of a Fit point in Vantage Fit?

Reward values vary by region and are set during account configuration. Your account manager confirms the local points-to-currency rate before your program launches.
